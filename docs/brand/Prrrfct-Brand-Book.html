01 — Brand essence

A quiet joy, every day.

Prrrfct is an AI-powered virtual cat companion. We exist to create emotional connection through daily care — not to optimize, gamify, or distract.

Caring for your AI-powered furry friend brings a relaxing moment of joy to your day.

Positioning line · prrrfct.com

Personality

01

Calm

Unhurried interfaces. Generous space. No urgency, no badges shouting for attention. The product should feel like sitting down — not like opening a feed.

02

Warm

Coral, soft light, human language. We speak like a gentle companion, never like a platform. Warmth is the brand’s primary emotion.

03

Playful — but premium

Personality without gimmicks. A whisker of wit is welcome; stickers, chaos, and “fun-brands” energy are not. Polished first, charming second.

04

Caring

Feed, groom, pet, play — framed as connection, not chores or streaks. Care is the product. Accessibility is not an afterthought; it is how care is delivered.

Voice

We sound like

  • Short, warm sentences. Room to breathe.
  • “Your companion.” “A quiet moment.” “When you’re ready.”
  • Emotional connection over utility and feature lists.
  • Inclusive, plain language. Accessibility-first by default.
  • Present tense, close to the user — never corporate.

We never sound like

  • Hype: “revolutionary,” “disrupt,” “the future of pets.”
  • Gamified pressure, FOMO, or streak-shaming.
  • Sarcasm, chaos, or tech-bro punchlines.
  • Infantilizing the user or over-cutesy copy.
  • Dark patterns around care loops or notifications.

Vision

A daily ritual of care that leaves people calmer than it found them — powered by AI, felt as companionship.

Promise

Emotional connection over utility. Premium-friendly, polished UX. Launching December 2025.

Audience

Anyone who wants a gentle companion in the day. Designed to be usable, readable, and kind to every body and mind.

02 — Logo

The wordmark is the brand.

A hand-drawn script that reads as a purr and a signature. Use it with restraint. It is already expressive — it does not need decoration.

Primary lockups

Do

Keep original proportions

Scale uniformly. The script’s rhythm is the identity — never stretch, squash, or redraw letterforms.

Two colors only

Ink #111111 on light surfaces. White on dark surfaces. No other fills — including Brand 50.

Give it air

Clear space on all sides equals at least one-quarter of the wordmark’s height (¼ ×).

Clear space

¼ × ¼ × ¼ × ¼ ×

Minimum clear space is one-quarter of the wordmark height, measured from the outermost paths. No type, icons, or edges may enter this zone.

Don’t

Don’t

Don’t stretchNever scale on a single axis, skew, or compress the script to fit a layout.

Don’t

Don’t recolorExcept white on dark. Coral, gradients, and off-black fills are off-brand.

Don’t

Don’t add effectsNo drop shadows, outlines, glows, bevels, rotation, or containers around the mark.

Favicon & app icon

Use the provided favicon as-is for browser chrome and small product surfaces. Do not redraw or recolor.

07 — Imagery

Soft light. Real cats. No circus.

Photography should feel like a quiet room in late afternoon. The hero poster is the north star: warm subject, cool restful ground, eye contact, no clutter.

Hero poster · hero-poster.jpg · reference for all companion photography

Seek

  • Soft, directional lighting — window light, not flash
  • Cats at rest or in gentle eye contact
  • Cozy materials: linen, wool, wood, warm fur
  • Shallow depth of field; uncluttered backgrounds
  • Color that can sit beside coral without competing

Avoid

  • Harsh contrast, neon, or high-saturation filters
  • Chaotic rooms, party energy, meme crops
  • Anthropomorphized costumes or props
  • Stock “happy pet owner” clichés and forced smiles
  • Busy collages, duotones, or heavy text on the face

Light

Diffuse and kind. Catchlights in the eyes. Shadows soft enough to feel like indoor day.

Palette in frame

Ginger, cream, taupe, cool grey. Let Brand 50 live in the UI, not as a photo grade.

Crop

Give the subject space to look into. Prefer portraits and near-portraits over wide environmental shots.
